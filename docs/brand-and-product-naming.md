# Hephzibah Behulah — Product naming and identity standard

Status: Approved naming direction (Adigun product family), 30 October 2026.

## Brand hierarchy
**Hephzibah Behulah** (corporate owner) → **HB division** (business category) → **Adigun[Product]** (product).

Every new software product should have a descriptive Adigun-prefixed name, e.g. AdigunSimulator (HB Elektroniker). Names for future products are proposals until individually approved; check name collisions, domains and trademarks before release. Do not silently rename existing applications, repositories, packages or URLs.

## One-logo rule
Use **one approved corporate Hephzibah Behulah logo** in the product's main header. The product name is a text wordmark using the approved design-system typography. The division is identified by a small textual label (e.g. BY HB ELEKTRONIKER) and its approved accent color. No extra competing division/product logos in the main header. An optional product icon is permitted for favicons, application launchers and other icon-specific contexts; it must follow the shared identity system. Adigun BOT has a consistent assistant icon, not an additional brand logo.

Do not invent or replace an approved corporate logo. The source file and official font have to be located and verified in the design-system assets before production use. Until then, use a clearly identified text fallback rather than fabricate an official mark.

## Shared layout
Header: single corporate logo + product wordmark + subtle division label + actual product navigation + theme and four-language selectors where implemented.
Footer: corporate ownership, division and product names in text, verified company website/social profiles, working documentation/support links, current year.
Floating assistant: Adigun BOT across products, integrated through product-specific validated tools and knowledge.

Mandatory languages: English (en), German (de), Yoruba (yo), Nigerian Pidgin (pcm). Use complete translated strings, not just a selector.
